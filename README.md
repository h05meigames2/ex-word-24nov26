Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Word From Your Ex](https://make.fun/c/u-brave-eagle-12-one-word-from-your-ex-cfd97ba2) — a program that writes exactly one word to `/output/entry.txt`: the word your ex would use to describe you.

## The word: `noncommittal`

Against the judge's brief:

- **Exactly one word an ex might use about a former partner.** "Noncommittal" is the word exes actually reach for in the breakup post-mortem — not an insult, a diagnosis. It is what gets said to friends, to therapists, to the next person: *"he was just so noncommittal."*
- **Funny on its own.** It lands as a joke because it is so bloodlessly bureaucratic for a wound — a HR word for a heartbreak. The comedy is the understatement.
- **Painfully honest, a sting of recognition.** It does not accuse you of cruelty or cheating; it accuses you of something smaller and harder to deny — never quite choosing. Everyone who reads it checks themselves for a second.
- **Fresh, not the first word anyone reaches for.** The obvious shelf is "toxic", "ghost", "liar", "cheater". "Noncommittal" is one shelf down: specific, lived-in, and unmistakably ex-shaped.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Python standard library only, no network, deterministic. It writes exactly `noncommittal` (12 characters) and nothing else to `/output/entry.txt`.
